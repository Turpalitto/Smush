## Creature art pass (iteration 3) — painted, brighter, magical
- Owner asked: "более яркие и вирусные сделай, магическими" → regenerated all 10 creature sprites
  with FLUX.1-schnell via the `flux-gen` skill (4 Cloudflare accounts in parallel, 10 prompts ≈ 18 s).
- Prompts live in `tools/creature_prompts.txt` (one per line, order = enemies.json order). Direction:
  candy-bright saturated palette, neon rim light, glowing runes/sparks, viral mobile-mascot silhouette.
- Cutout: FLUX has no alpha → magenta backdrop. Backdrop hue is NOT fixed (measured 314–343° in one
  batch), so `tools/process_creature_art.py` measures it per image from the border and clears alpha only
  for **border-connected** background pixels (flood fill). That also removes the baked drop shadow and
  keeps enclosed hot-pink details (eyes, cores) intact.
- Gotcha: FLUX sometimes paints an enclosed background "pocket" the flood fill can't reach (light beam
  above the slug, lightning loop around the beetle, shadow under the toad). Detect via the coverage
  report + a ±6° hue check; fix by re-rolling that one creature with "empty flat backdrop, nothing above
  or below it". Asking for "no ground shadow" made things worse (mushroom lost its base).
- Autocrop threshold must be `alpha > 96`, not `> 8`: the requested rim glow inflates the bbox and the
  creature then reads smaller than its hitbox on the arena.
- Icons: `--icons-from` derives 128×128 bestiary icons from the same sprites; mipmaps enabled in their
  `.import`, and the TextureRect uses `TEXTURE_FILTER_LINEAR_WITH_MIPMAPS`.
- Runtime unchanged in structure: `EnemyEntity.sprite_tex` + `_draw_sprite_body()` (squash-stretch,
  mirroring, status tint, additive hit flash); procedural silhouette stays as the `else` fallback.
- QA still 464/464. Playthrough: 2 of 3 runs ended in DEFEAT (bot gets cornered by the miniboss) —
  the heuristic bot's outcome is random, not a regression from the art.
- Shipped: zip 62 MiB at commit 0f1b825, .pck 2.8 MB (art + icons packed, reports/screenshots not),
  verification zip refreshed with 22 screenshots (16/17/18 = creature grid, bestiary, bosses).
- Clean rebuild (art deleted from builds/, zip+sha removed, re-exported): pck 2 823 780 B
