# Order: singing_crystal, dew_fountain, ancient_lantern
#
# Why these exist: the forest is infinite and beautiful but it has nothing in it that
# is a *destination*. Every sprite in the other sets is scenery — it repeats, it is
# never the reason you walked that way. These three are placed on their own lattice
# (WorldGenerator._wonder_in_cell), they wake once, they chime, they heal and they
# shed spores, and their halo is visible from a screen away so a player drifting in a
# flow state has something to drift toward.
#
# Three different silhouettes on purpose: a vertical prism, a wide low basin and a
# tall thin lantern. From a screen away and half in the fog, shape is the only thing
# that identifies them.
#
# The lantern needs --key-mode color, not the default flood fill: its vine handle is a
# closed loop, so the flood fill cannot reach the backdrop trapped inside it and left a
# magenta wedge in the hole. That is why the lantern is processed on its own, in its own
# directory, with --key-mode color (see the commands above).
#
# No ground shadow: the game draws its own contact shadow from the sprite's footprint,
# and a baked one would sit at the wrong angle under the slime's moving light.
